ROLLING REWARD — WHAT THE NUMBER MEANS
=======================================

The "Rolling reward" reported at every check is `rollout/ep_rew_mean`
from Stable-Baselines3:

  Rolling reward = mean total reward over the last 100 completed
                   training episodes (a sliding window).

Each "episode" is one full Breakout game (start to game-over after all
5 lives are lost). Each episode produces one reward number, which is
the total points scored in that game. As a new episode ends it enters
the window and the oldest one drops out.

Properties to keep in mind:

  - Lags real-time performance.
    The window contains episodes from ~100 games ago. If the policy
    just improved 10 minutes back, only a handful of post-improvement
    episodes are in the window — the rest still reflect the old policy.

  - Smooths out individual luck.
    One bad game (say, 12 points) vs one good one (40 points) is just
    noise; the rolling average tells us the policy's typical level.

  - Sees exploration noise.
    During training the policy is stochastic; the agent occasionally
    tries non-greedy actions. So the rolling reward is consistently
    a few points LOWER than the eval reward.

Two distinct reward metrics in this log:

  - Rolling reward (continuous during training)
    What the checkpoints in the tables below report. Source:
    `rollout/ep_rew_mean` line in the SB3 stdout.

  - Eval reward / Tier reward (only at eval_freq=100,000 steps)
    Mean of 10 DETERMINISTIC episodes (no exploration noise) used by
    the EvolutionCallback to decide if a tier has been crossed. Source:
    `eval/mean_reward` in the SB3 stdout. Generally a few points higher
    than the rolling reward at the same moment.

Example from our run: at step 1.7M the eval reported +30.2 (Skilled tier
saved), while the rolling reward at that same step was +26.1. Both are
correct — they measure different things.

REWARD TRAJECTORY PATTERN
==========================

Phase 1 (0 -> 100k): Random/lucky play
    Reward 0-6. Auto-fire mechanic + random paddle motion gives ~5 reward
    by chance. The Learning tier was reached on the first eval.

Phase 2 (100k -> 400k): Rapid early learning
    Reward climbed 6 -> 20 in ~300k steps. Agent learned the basics:
    track the ball, hit it back, score on bottom rows.

Phase 3 (400k -> 1.4M): The plateau
    Classic Breakout flat zone. Reward hovered 22-25 with very slow gains
    (+0.3 to +0.7 per 100k steps). This is the published Breakout PPO
    behavior — agent has the easy points and is searching for strategy.

Phase 4 (1.4M -> 3.4M): Brief climb then second plateau
    Reward gradient picked back up (+1.0 to +2.6 per 100k). Skilled tier
    fired at 1.7M steps with reward +30.2. Rolling avg crossed +30
    briefly at 3.1M but pulled back.

Phase 5 (3.4M -> 4.5M): Locked plateau at +28-29
    Ten consecutive checks oscillated between +28 and +30. Mean ~28.5,
    std ~1.4. The agent had truly converged on this local optimum.

Phase 6 (4.5M -> 5.4M): Brief plateau exit before restart
    Reward jumped to +33 between two checks. Likely the start of an
    upward break, but the run was stopped at 5,385,728 steps to apply
    a higher entropy bonus (see Run 2).

Phase 7 (Run 2, 3.8M -> 6.8M): Exploration dip and recovery
    First ~3M steps of Run 2. Reward dipped from +27 down to +24.6
    (bottom at R2-3) and oscillated +24-28 for ~10 checks. The agent
    was sampling new behaviours; reward variance was higher than Run 1
    but the mean stayed slightly below Run 1's plateau.

Phase 8 (6.8M -> ongoing): Higher floor, no tunnel yet
    Starting around R2-13 (step 6.86M) reward broke into the +30-32
    band and has held there since. Mean of last several checks: ~+30.6,
    which is ~+2 above Run 1's plateau. The exploration lifted the
    floor a couple of points but has NOT unlocked the tunnel strategy.
    At step 10.2M (halfway to the 20M target) the agent is still
    operating at Skilled-tier level with no Expert-tier breakthrough.

WHAT TRIGGERED EACH TIER SAVE
==============================

The EvolutionCallback (callbacks.py) only saves a checkpoint the FIRST
time the agent enters a skill band. The save criterion is the mean of 10
evaluation episodes at eval_freq=100,000 steps. Filename format:

  tier_<name>_t<steps>_r<reward>.zip

Each save also records 2 gameplay episodes as MP4 video using the
deterministic policy at that point.


RUN 2 — RESTART WITH HIGHER EXPLORATION
========================================

Why we restarted
-----------------
After ~20 hours of Run 1 training, the agent had clearly converged at the
Skilled tier (+28-33) with no sign of finding the tunnel strategy. The
published behavior of vanilla PPO on Breakout is to plateau here unless
the entropy coefficient is tuned higher to keep exploration alive.

The decision: pause Run 1 with the best model preserved, raise ent_coef
from 0.01 to 0.02, and resume. This trades a few hours of temporary reward
dip (the agent re-explores) for a meaningfully higher chance of breaking
into the Expert tier.

Estimated probabilities (from published PPO Breakout literature):
  Vanilla ent_coef=0.01  ->  ~20% chance of tunnel discovery before 20M steps
  ent_coef=0.02          ->  ~50% chance of tunnel discovery before 20M steps

Changes applied
----------------
  config.py
    PPO_CONFIG["ent_coef"]  0.01  ->  0.02
    STOP_AT_TIER            None  ->  "expert"   (new key)

  callbacks.py
    EvolutionCallback now accepts stop_at_tier= parameter.
    When the saved tier matches stop_at_tier, _on_step() returns False
    on the next call, ending training cleanly.

  train.py
    On --resume, model.ent_coef is overridden from the current config
    (PPO.load would otherwise restore the saved 0.01 value).
    EvolutionCallback receives stop_at_tier from config.STOP_AT_TIER.

What to expect
---------------
  First 30 min - 2 hours:  Reward may dip below +30 as the agent explores
  Hours 18-40:             Realistic window for tunnel-strategy breakthrough
  On breakthrough:         Reward can jump from ~30 to 100+ in a single hour
                           tier_expert_*.zip + video will be saved
                           Auto-stop kicks in immediately after that save

If no breakthrough by 20M total steps, training exits normally and we
still have a Skilled-tier agent (already preserved in best_model.zip).

HOW TO RESUME WORK  (UPDATED 2026-06-03 — training now runs under systemd)
===================

Training is managed by the `breakout-train.service` user service, not by
manual nohup/kill — control it with the systemctl commands below.

  systemctl --user status  breakout-train.service
  systemctl --user stop    breakout-train.service     # clean stop
  systemctl --user restart breakout-train.service     # apply config changes
  # resume from a specific model once:
  echo models/best_model.zip > models/.resume_override
  systemctl --user restart breakout-train.service

To watch saved versions play:
  python3 play.py --tier skilled
  python3 play.py --compare           # split-screen first vs best
  python3 play.py --all               # render every tier in sequence
