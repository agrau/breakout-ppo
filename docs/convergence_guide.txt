BREAKOUT PPO — CONVERGENCE GUIDE
=================================

The training curves saved to plots/training_curves.png give a full picture
of whether the agent is learning correctly. Here is how to read each panel.


SIGNS THE TRAINING IS CONVERGING
---------------------------------

Mean Episode Reward
    Rises from ~0 toward 100+ and then flattens out (or keeps climbing
    toward 400 if the agent learns the tunnel-digging strategy).

Reward (smoothed)
    Same trend as above but averaged over 30 updates, so the signal is
    cleaner. This is the most reliable convergence indicator.

Policy Loss
    Oscillates near 0 without drifting away in either direction.

Policy Entropy
    Gradually decreases over time. The agent becomes more decisive as it
    learns a good strategy.

Value Loss
    Decreases and stabilizes. The critic (value network) is learning to
    accurately predict future rewards.

Clip Fraction
    Stays consistently in the 0.05 to 0.15 range. This means PPO's clipping
    mechanism is keeping policy updates at a safe size.


SIGNS OF PROBLEMS
-----------------

Reward stuck at 0-2 for many million steps
    The agent never learned to FIRE the ball at episode start. Breakout
    requires pressing FIRE to launch — without it the episode times out
    with no score. SB3's AtariWrapper handles this via FireResetEnv, so it
    should not happen, but if it does, double-check the wrapper stack.

Reward rises then suddenly collapses
    Catastrophic forgetting. The policy overshot and destroyed what it had
    learned. Try lowering INITIAL_LR or reducing clip_range.

Entropy drops to near 0 early in training
    The agent got stuck in a bad deterministic policy (often: always-FIRE
    or always-NOOP) before finding a good one. Raise ent_coef in
    config.py to encourage more exploration.

Value Loss keeps growing instead of stabilizing
    The critic is diverging. Try lowering vf_coef or INITIAL_LR.

Clip Fraction above 0.3 consistently
    Policy updates are too large. Lower INITIAL_LR or reduce clip_range.


EXPECTED LEARNING TIMELINE (20M steps on Breakout)
---------------------------------------------------

Steps 0 - 2M     Reward flat near 0-2. The agent plays randomly and is
                 just starting to learn that FIRE launches the ball.

Steps 2M - 6M    Reward climbs toward 10-30. The agent reliably hits the
                 ball back and clears the bottom rows of bricks.

Steps 6M - 12M   Reward enters the 30-100 range. The agent clears most
                 of the wall in a typical episode.

Steps 12M - 20M  Reward approaches 200+ and sometimes 400+. The agent has
                 discovered the "tunnel" strategy: clearing one vertical
                 column to send the ball above the wall where it bounces
                 around scoring many points without needing the paddle.

If the smoothed reward is still under 5 after 5M steps, something is wrong
with the training setup rather than it just being too early to see
progress.


WHY BREAKOUT IS HARDER THAN PONG
---------------------------------

Pong gives a reward signal every few seconds (each point scored). Breakout's
reward signal is much sparser: the agent only gets a reward when it hits a
brick, and the lower bricks (1 point) appear early but the upper bricks (7
points) only become reachable after the wall is partially cleared.

This sparse-reward structure is why Breakout needs ~2-5× more steps than
Pong to reach expert play.


KEY PARAMETER REFERENCE (config.py)
-------------------------------------

INITIAL_LR      2.5e-4   Learning rate, anneals linearly to 0.
ent_coef        0.01     Entropy bonus. RL-Zoo recipe value. Raising it
                         (tried 0.04) only lifts a stuck policy slightly then
                         re-plateaus AND blocks convergence to tunneling —
                         keep at 0.01 for the exploit/converge phase.
clip_range      0.1→0    Anneals linearly to 0 (lin_0.1). Set in build_model
                         for fresh runs and re-applied on resume.
vf_coef         0.5      Weight of the value loss in the total loss.
n_steps         128      Rollout length per environment per update.
n_epochs        4        PPO epochs per rollout.
batch_size      256      Minibatch size.
N_ENVS          4        Parallel environments (benchmark uses 8; reduced
                         here for the Jetson's RAM/OOM limit).
TOTAL_TIMESTEPS 20M      Benchmark hits ~398 raw by 10M; 20M gives margin.

This config matches the proven RL-Zoo PPO Breakout recipe
(huggingface.co/sb3/ppo-BreakoutNoFrameskip-v4), which reaches ~398.


MEASURING THE TRUE SCORE (clipped-per-life vs raw full-game)
-------------------------------------------------------------
In-training eval (progress.csv, evaluations.npz, tiers) reports CLIPPED
reward per LIFE (each brick = +1; episode = 1 life), because make_atari_env
applies ClipRewardEnv + EpisodicLifeEnv. The published "~398" benchmark is
RAW score over a full 5-life game — a different scale, NOT comparable.

To get a benchmark-comparable number, run eval_raw.py (clip_reward=False,
terminal_on_life_loss=False) — but ONLY while the trainer is stopped (two
PPO processes exhaust this box's RAM). Note: in a full game the agent must
press FIRE to relaunch after each lost life; a weak policy stalls there, so
the raw number can read pessimistically low.
